Delete your account

You can delete your WannaGame account yourself, any time, from inside the app.

  1. Open WannaGame and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with Delete account.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, email address, username and profile picture
  • Your calls, and your answers to other people’s calls
  • Your squad memberships and push notification tokens
  • Squads you own that have nobody else in them, including their pictures

What stays

  • Squads you own that have other members pass to the member who joined first, so your friends keep their squad.

Deletion happens right away and can’t be undone. Nothing is kept afterwards.

Can’t get into the app?

Email privacy@wannagame.stream from the address you signed up with and we’ll delete the account for you. See also the Privacy Policy.
