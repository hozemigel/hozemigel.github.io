Child safety standards

Last updated: 21 October 2026.

WannaGame is a small app for asking your friends “wanna play?”. It is for people aged 16 and over. We do not allow child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) or child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form, and we act on it whenever we learn about it.

What is not allowed

  • Any sexual content involving a minor, or any attempt to groom, sexualise or exploit a minor.
  • Pictures, gamer tags, names or squad names that are sexual, or that sexualise children.
  • Using WannaGame to contact, threaten or pressure a minor.

How the app keeps this small

  • There is no public feed, no search for strangers and no chat. You only see people who are in a squad with you.
  • Nobody is added to a squad without asking: you join with an invite link, or you accept an invitation. You can switch off invitations by gamer tag in Profile.
  • You can report and block anyone in a squad (Members → ⋯ → Report or Block).

Reporting

Report a person in the app (Members → ⋯ → Report), or email us at privacy@wannagame.stream. Reports about child safety are looked at first.

What we do

  • We remove the content and the account that broke these rules.
  • We keep what we need as evidence, and we report CSAM and CSAE to the proper authorities, such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the local police, as the law requires.
  • We comply with the child safety laws that apply to us.

Contact

Child safety contact: privacy@wannagame.stream. See also the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy.
