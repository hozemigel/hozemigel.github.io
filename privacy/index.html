Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026.

WannaGame lets you ask your friends “wanna play?” with one tap. This page explains what we collect to make that work, and what we do with it. We don’t sell your data and we don’t show ads.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address (or your Google account email if you sign in with Google), your gamer tag, and optionally a display name.
  • Pictures: your profile picture and squad pictures, only if you choose to upload them.
  • Squads and calls: the squads you create or join, their games, the calls you send, and your answers (“I’m in” / “I’ll pass”). Other members of your squad can see these.
  • Push notifications: a notification token for your device, so we can tell you when a friend calls. You can turn notifications off in Profile.
  • Settings: things like your call sound, muted squads and quiet hours.

What we don’t collect

  • No location, contacts, microphone, camera recordings or advertising ID.
  • No analytics or tracking of what you do in the app.

Who can see what

Your gamer tag, display name and picture are visible to the people in your squads, and to anyone who has a squad’s invite link (they see the squad’s name, picture and games, not its members). Your email is never shown to other users.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores your account and data and sends sign-in codes.
  • Resend delivers the email with your sign-in code.
  • Google (Sign-In and Firebase Cloud Messaging) if you sign in with Google, and to deliver push notifications on Android.
  • Expo relays push notifications.
  • IGDB provides game names and covers. When you search for a game, only the search text is sent, never anything about you.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Calls and answers are kept as part of your squad’s history while the squad exists.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account yourself in the app (Profile → Delete account). Everything tied to you is removed right away. Details are on the delete account page. You can also email us and we’ll do it for you.

Children

WannaGame is not meant for anyone under 16, and we don’t knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we’ll update the date above and tell you in the app.

Contact
